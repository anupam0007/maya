FAQ

Questions clients ask.

How long does a project take?

Invitations and logos usually take 2–5 days. Brochures take 3–7 days. A business website takes 1–3 weeks, and a full online store 4–8 weeks, depending on the number of products and features.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. Book a free call with a few details and we'll follow up with a clear, itemised quote within a day — no obligation.

What happens on the call?

It's a relaxed 30 minutes on Google Meet. You tell us what you're planning, we ask a few questions and show you similar work we've done. You'll leave with a clear idea of timeline and cost. No sales script.

Can you design invitations in Kannada?

Yes. We design invitations in Kannada, English or both, as images, PDFs or animated video invitations with music.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can refresh the design, speed it up, make it mobile-friendly, or rebuild it completely while keeping your content.

Do you help after the project is delivered?

Yes. For websites we help with updates, fixes and small changes after launch. For designs, you can always come back for new versions or sizes.

Do you work with clients outside Bengaluru?

Yes. Everything can be done over Google Meet, WhatsApp, calls and email, so we work with clients across India and abroad.
